2. What Bail does

Bail locks apps, app categories and websites you pick (your inmates) for a set time (a sentence). A sentence ends in one of these ways:

  • Served: its time is up.
  • Paroled: if you chose parole as the way out, you solve its challenges.

You can also choose no bail: then only time ends the sentence.

Bail runs on Apple's Screen Time. It works only while Bail has Screen Time access on your iPhone.

3. Sentence rules

  • When you hold to lock up, the door takes about 3 seconds to shut. Until it does, Wait, undo cancels the sentence. Nothing is locked.
  • Once the door has shut, a sentence can only get stricter. You can extend it or add inmates. You can't shorten it, remove inmates or delete it. Only its way out or time ends it.
  • Visits are open times inside a sentence. During a visit your apps are free, but the sentence stands: no edits, no early end. You can remove a visit later. You can't add one.
  • Repeats lock the same inmates on a weekly schedule. While a repeat is open, you can edit or delete it. While it's locked, nothing about it can change.
  • Stricter now, softer tomorrow. Settings that make Bail stricter apply at once. Settings that relax it apply later: strict mode once nothing is locked.

4. Escapes

While a sentence is active, each of these counts as an escape, also called a jailbreak:

  1. deleting Bail;
  2. switching off Bail's Screen Time access;
  3. erasing or resetting your iPhone.

A sentence is active from the moment the door shuts until it is served or paroled, its visits included. A repeat is active while it is locked. This is the whole list.

What an escape costs:

  • The sentence goes on your rap sheet as escaped, and your clean streak resets.
  • The escaped sentence waits for you. When you come back, you serve it again in full before you can lock up anything new.

Bail recognises an escaped sentence by a small marker it keeps in your iPhone's Keychain, as far as iOS keeps it.

5. Strict mode

Strict mode is on by default. While a sentence is active, it has iOS refuse to delete any app, Bail included, and keep the date and time set automatically. Switching it off applies once nothing is locked.

Common questions

I tapped Ask for parole on the shield. Nothing happened.

iOS won't let a locked app open Bail, so Bail sends a notification. Check that notifications are on for Bail in iOS Settings. Or open Bail within 5 minutes: it opens on the parole hearing.

Time's up. My apps are still locked.

Open Bail. It releases whatever is due when it opens.

Can I shorten a sentence?

No. Once the door shuts, only parole or time gets them out. You can make it longer or add inmates.

Why can't I delete apps?

Strict mode. It's on by default and stops apps from being deleted while a sentence runs. Switch it off in Bail's Settings: with nothing locked it goes off at once, otherwise once nothing is.

Why can't I change the date and time?

Strict mode again. While a sentence runs, the date and time stay set automatically. They are yours to change again once nothing is locked.

Does anything about my apps leave my iPhone?

No. Apple gives Bail anonymous tokens, and they stay on your iPhone. More in the privacy policy.
